Refund policy

This payment is mostly a hold so we can badge you and issue kit. $0.00 comes back after you complete your first assigned shift.

  1. Your kit or uniform deposit is refunded when issued gear is returned in usable condition.
  2. Your role briefing amount is credited back as a first-shift completion rebate.
  3. We send that refund to the same Chime account you paid from, usually within 2 business days of the completed shift.
  4. If we cancel your placement before you work, we return the full amount you paid.

Credentialing and clearance stay as processing so we can register you for the venue. Please confirm the recipient details carefully before sending payment.
